What drives me

My Values

The principles and beliefs that guide how I work and how I live.

Making an Impact

I'm not interested in just checking boxes. I want my work to actually protect people and systems, and leave things better than I found them.

Better Together

My best work happens on teams with a clear mission. I thrive when everyone knows what they're working toward and why it matters.

Do It Right

Reliability means more than showing up — it means following through, sweating the details, and not taking shortcuts when it counts.

Give Back

Community isn't just a word to me. I volunteer at my local food pantry because showing up for the people around me matters just as much as the work I do professionally.

Never Stop Growing

15 years in and I'm still chasing certifications, learning new tools, and picking up the bass. Curiosity doesn't have an off switch.

Built to Create

Whether it's writing music, playing the bass, or designing a cleaner security architecture — I find real satisfaction in making something from nothing.

"Tell me and I forget. Teach me and I remember. Involve me and I learn."

— Benjamin Franklin
